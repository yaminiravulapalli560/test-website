Resolving Production Regression Issues in E-Commerce Platforms.

In this case study, we develop into the challenges faced by a team responsible for maintaining an e-commerce platform.

These issues range from discrepancies in invoice product names to malfunctioning login pop-ups and brand page loading errors.
Each team member has distinct responsibilities and faces unique challenges in resolving these issues promptly and effectively.
